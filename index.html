About US work authorization. On an F-1 visa you can usually do a US summer internship on CPT without employer sponsorship. What matters is whether the firm will sponsor an H-1B for a full-time return offer. The badges show:
  • Sponsors MBA hires The firm is known to sponsor MBA hires. Still confirm for your specific office.
  • Not stated · ask The posting doesn't say. Ask the recruiter before you invest time.
  • No sponsorship The posting rules out sponsorship. These cards are hidden by default.
  • China work eligibility China roles generally require Chinese citizenship or a valid China work permit.

How to use this

  • Expected MBB first-year MBA deadlines fall in November 2026, and many schools set their own dates. Your school's career office (e.g., Yale SOM CDO) will have the exact dates for your program.
  • For China postings, also check the employer's WeChat campus-recruiting (校园招聘) account. Many China MBA intern roles are posted there first.
  • "Careers portal" cards mean no 2027 MBA posting was confirmed yet. Bookmark the page and check back.
